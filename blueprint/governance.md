# Governance

## One Platform

All products belong to one ecosystem.

Products must strengthen the platform.

---

## Reuse Before Build

Before creating anything new, ask:

Can it be reused?

Can it become shared?

---

## Architecture First

Never write code before defining architecture.

---

## Documentation First

Major decisions must be documented.

---

## Standard First

Every product follows SES.

No exception.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Standards may evolve,

but every change must be documented.

---

## Long-term Thinking

Choose solutions that remain maintainable

five years from now,

not only today.
